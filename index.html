Cardiovascular Disease Prediction

A public dataset project that uses routine health indicators to predict recorded cardiovascular disease with logistic regression. The work covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, model evaluation and a prototype checker that communicates the results clearly. The selected model achieved 81.6% recall and 0.795 ROC-AUC on the test set.
